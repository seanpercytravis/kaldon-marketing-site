## The Typical 12-App Shopify Stack and Monthly Cost

Here is what a profitable Shopify store doing $50,000 to $200,000 per month in revenue typically pays for apps. These are real market prices as of June 2026.

### 1. Reviews and Social Proof
- **Judge.me Plus or Loox Premium**: $15 to $34/month
- **Function**: Photo reviews, widgets, email requests
- **Why stores pay**: 4.7+ star rating and 50+ reviews are table stakes for conversion

### 2. Email and SMS Marketing
- **Klaviyo or Omnisend**: $60 to $700/month depending on list size and SMS volume
- **Function**: Segmentation, abandoned cart, post-purchase flows, SMS campaigns
- **Why stores pay**: Email typically drives 25% to 40% of revenue for established stores
- **Pricing trap**: Klaviyo charges per contact and per SMS sent. A 15,000-contact list with moderate SMS usage costs $150 to $300/month. Heavy SMS users hit $500+/month.

### 3. Subscription Management
- **Recharge Standard, Bold, Skio, or Yotpo Subscriptions**: $49 to $99/month base, often plus 0.75% to 1.25% transaction fee on subscription revenue
- **Function**: Recurring billing, customer portal, churn reduction tools
- **Why stores pay**: Subscription revenue has higher LTV and lower CAC
- **Pricing trap**: A store doing $20,000/month in subscription revenue pays $49 base plus $150 to $250 in transaction fees, totaling $200 to $300/month for the app alone.

### 4. Post-Purchase Upsells and Bundles
- **Rebuy, AfterSell, or Zipify OneClickUpsell**: $99 to $499/month, often tiered by order volume
- **Function**: Thank-you page upsells, cart widgets, product bundles
- **Why stores pay**: 5% to 15% AOV lift when executed well
- **Pricing trap**: Per-order pricing means a good month doubles your bill with no warning.

### 5. Helpdesk and Customer Support
- **Gorgias or Richpanel**: $10 to $300/month depending on ticket volume
- **Function**: Unified inbox for email, SMS, social, live chat with Shopify integrations
- **Why stores pay**: Without it, customer service lives in 4+ tabs and misses orders

### 6. Shipping and Tracking
- **AfterShip or Wonderment**: $9 to $99/month, often with per-shipment fees starting at $0.05 to $0.10 per tracking event
- **Function**: Branded tracking pages, delivery notifications, reduce WISMO tickets
- **Why stores pay**: Cuts support volume 15% to 30%
- **Pricing trap**: At 2,000 orders/month, a $0.06/shipment fee adds $120/month on top of the base plan.

### 7. Returns and Exchanges
- **Loop Returns or Returnly**: $59 to $299/month, often plus $1 to $2 per return processed
- **Function**: Self-service return portal, exchange incentives, refund automation
- **Why stores pay**: Manual returns destroy margin on support time and delay refunds
- **Pricing trap**: A 5% return rate on 3,000 orders/month is 150 returns. At $1.20 per return, that is $180/month in per-return fees plus the base plan.

### 8. Loyalty and Referrals
- **Yotpo Loyalty, Smile.io, or LoyaltyLion**: $49 to $199/month
- **Function**: Points, VIP tiers, referral programs
- **Why stores pay**: Repeat purchase rate lifts 10% to 20% with a good program

### 9. Page Builder or Landing Pages
- **Shogun, GemPages, or PageFly**: $39 to $149/month
- **Function**: Custom landing pages, product pages, no-code design
- **Why stores pay**: Theme builders are limited for custom layouts and A/B tests

### 10. Analytics and Attribution
- **Northbeam, Triple Whale, or Polar Analytics**: $50 to $500/month
- **Function**: Multi-touch attribution, LTV cohorts, channel ROI
- **Why stores pay**: Shopify native analytics do not show true CAC or incremental ROAS by channel

### 11. Popups and Conversion Rate Optimization
- **Justuno, Privy, or OptiMonk**: $19 to $79/month
- **Function**: Exit intent, email capture, gamified spin-to-win
- **Why stores pay**: 2% to 5% of site visitors convert to email subscribers

### 12. Inventory and Order Management (for multi-channel sellers)
- **SkuVault, Cin7, or Sellbrite**: $50 to $500/month
- **Function**: Sync inventory across Shopify, Amazon, Walmart, eBay
- **Why stores pay**: Manual stock updates cause oversells and chargebacks

### Total Monthly App Cost
For a mid-market Shopify store:
- **Low end** (starter plans, minimal SMS/subscriptions): $400 to $700/month
- **Mid-market** (established brand, 2,000+ orders/month): $800 to $1,500/month
- **High end** (heavy email/SMS, subscriptions, multi-channel): $1,500 to $2,500+/month

These numbers do not include Shopify plan fees ($39 to $399/month for core plans, $2,300+/month for Plus), transaction fees, or payment processing.

Operators on Reddit report that app fees often exceed their Shopify plan by 3x to 5x. One founder shared: "I pay $39 for Shopify and over $250 for apps. Everyone said Shopify was cheap, but every feature is an extra $10 to $50/mo."

## The Hidden Costs: Per-Order Fees and Revenue Share

Many Shopify apps have shifted from flat monthly pricing to usage-based models in the last 18 months. This creates unpredictable bills that scale faster than revenue.

### Subscription App Transaction Fees
Most subscription apps charge 0.75% to 1.25% of subscription revenue on top of the base monthly fee. For a store doing $30,000/month in subscription revenue:
- **Recharge Standard**: $49/month + 1.25% = $49 + $375 = $424/month
- **Skio or Stay AI (enterprise tier)**: $199+/month + negotiated transaction fee

Buyers are now asking: "At what subscription MRV does a 'cheap' app with transaction fees cost more than a flat-fee competitor?"

The breakeven for most stores is around $10,000 to $15,000/month in subscription revenue. Below that, flat-fee tools like Appstle or Subify are cheaper. Above that, negotiate enterprise pricing.

### Per-Order and Per-Shipment Fees
Tracking, returns, and upsell apps often charge per event:
- **AfterShip**: $0.06 per tracked shipment
- **Loop Returns**: $1.20 per return processed
- **Rebuy**: Tiered by order volume, often $99 to $499/month

At 3,000 orders/month with a 5% return rate:
- Tracking: 3,000 shipments × $0.06 = $180/month
- Returns: 150 returns × $1.20 = $180/month
- Total per-event fees: $360/month on top of base subscriptions

One Reddit user shared: "One app doubled my bill when we had a good month. No email, nothing. Just a surprise charge. I deleted it immediately."

### The "App Tax" as Percent of Revenue
Industry benchmarks suggest healthy Shopify ecosystem spend (plan + apps + processing) should be 4% to 6% of revenue. Many established stores drift to 7% to 9%+ due to app sprawl.

For a store doing $100,000/month:
- **4% target**: $4,000/month (plan + apps + processing)
- **Typical reality**: $6,000 to $9,000/month once you add premium email, subscriptions, analytics, helpdesk, and per-order tools

The difference is $24,000 to $60,000 per year in margin.

## How to Audit and Cut Your Shopify App Stack

Here is how operators are consolidating their app spend in 2026:

### 1. Export Your Full App List and Costs
Shopify > Settings > Apps and Sales Channels > View detailed app charges. Export the last 3 months.

### 2. Tag Each App by Function and ROI
- **Must-have** (reviews, email, helpdesk): Keep but shop for cheaper equivalent
- **High ROI** (subscriptions, upsells that add measurable AOV): Keep
- **Low/unknown ROI** (popups, extra analytics, abandoned apps): Kill or trial removal
- **Overlapping** (2+ apps doing similar jobs): Consolidate

### 3. Replace 3 to 5 Simple Apps with Theme Code or Shopify Functions
Common targets:
- Announcement bars (theme feature in most premium themes)
- Simple volume discounts (Shopify discount functions)
- Order notes and custom fields (theme customization)
- Basic popups (email tool native forms)

Developers on Reddit frequently show how replacing 3 to 5 low-complexity apps with custom code cuts $50 to $150/month in recurring fees.

### 4. Negotiate or Switch to Flat-Fee Alternatives
For subscription, email, and analytics apps, compare:
- Current cost at your volume
- Competitor pricing at same volume
- Flat-fee vs usage-based breakeven

Example: If you are paying Klaviyo $300/month for a 20,000-contact list, compare Omnisend or Drip at the same tier. Switching can save $50 to $150/month.

### 5. Set a Target App Budget by Revenue
Use 3% to 5% of monthly revenue as your SaaS budget ceiling (apps only, not plan or processing).

For a $100,000/month store:
- **Target app spend**: $3,000 to $5,000/month
- **Typical reality**: $1,200 to $2,000/month for apps alone is healthy
- **Above $2,500/month**: Start cutting

One Reddit operator shared: "We're at $1,200/month in Shopify apps. Not bankrupting us, but it's stupid. Half these apps overlap. Our project this quarter is cutting the stack by at least a third."
