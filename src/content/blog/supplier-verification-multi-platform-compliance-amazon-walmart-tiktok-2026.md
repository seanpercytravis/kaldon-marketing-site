## Platform-Specific Supplier Documentation Requirements

### Amazon Supplier Verification Requirements 2026

Amazon enforces supplier verification through three systems: Account Health policy compliance, Product Safety dashboard requirements, and brand registry gating.

**Required supplier documents:**

- **Third-party lab test reports** (for supplements, topicals, electronics, children's products). As of January 2026, all dietary supplement sellers must provide third-party testing demonstrating compliance with FDCA labeling (21 CFR 101) and cGMP (21 CFR 111/117). High-risk categories (bodybuilding, sexual enhancement, sports nutrition, weight management) require NSF/ANSI 173-2023 testing.
- **cGMP audit certificate** (for supplements and food). Amazon requires accredited third-party audits, not self-certification.
- **FDA registration number and DUNS number** (for food and supplement imports). Manufacturing plants and exporter companies must be registered with FDA and have DUNS numbers before inventory enters FBA.
- **Supplier business license** (for Brand Registry and gated categories).
- **Invoice from manufacturer or authorized distributor** (to prove supply chain authenticity for IP claims).
- **ISO 9001 or equivalent quality management certificate** (for electronics and automotive categories).
- **INFORM Consumers Act identity verification** (for high-volume third-party sellers: bank account, contact details, Tax ID). Amazon requires video-based identity verification and recertifies annually.

**Enforcement mechanisms:**

- **Account Health → Policy Compliance → Food and Product Safety Issues → Verify Your Product.** Amazon routes flagged SKUs through this flow and requires you to select a testing or inspection provider and upload evidence.
- **AI-powered content moderation.** Amazon scans product titles, bullet points, and images for claims that trigger testing requirements. If your listing says "doctor-formulated" or "clinically tested" without corresponding lab reports, the SKU gets suppressed.
- **Inventory holds beyond 30 days.** The proposed federal Online Sellers' Bill of Rights would prohibit Amazon from holding seller inventory longer than 30 days without legally valid proof that goods are counterfeit or unlawful, but as of mid-2026, Amazon still exercises discretion on hold duration.

**Where verification gaps cost you:**

- **Delayed SKU approvals.** If you cannot provide a third-party test report within 72 hours of a compliance flag, the SKU stays suppressed and you lose Buy Box eligibility.
- **Fee leakage.** Amazon charges long-term storage fees and removal fees for inventory stuck in compliance review. One supplement brand reported $18,000 in removal and storage fees for 6,000 units held for 90 days while waiting for NSF test results.
- **Brand Registry rejection.** Without supplier invoices proving authorized distribution, your trademark application gets denied and competitors can hijack your listings.

### Walmart Supplier Verification Requirements 2026

Walmart Pro Seller and Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) enforce supplier verification through onboarding checklists, category-specific requirements, and ongoing compliance audits.

**Required supplier documents:**

- **W-9 form** (for U.S. suppliers) or **W-8BEN/W-8BEN-E** (for foreign suppliers). Walmart requires tax documentation at onboarding and annually.
- **General liability insurance certificate** (minimum $1 million per occurrence, $2 million aggregate, naming Walmart as additional insured). This is mandatory for Pro Seller approval.
- **Product liability insurance** (for high-risk categories: toys, electronics, supplements, topicals). Walmart may request proof during category application.
- **Lab test reports** (for supplements, cosmetics, children's products). Walmart accepts third-party testing from ISO 17025-accredited labs.
- **GS1 UPC certificate** (proving you own or licensed the barcode). Walmart rejects listings with non-GS1 barcodes or barcodes that do not match the product.
- **Supplier business license and articles of incorporation** (for Pro Seller onboarding).
- **CPSC tracking label and certificate of compliance** (for children's products). Walmart enforces this through its Product Safety team, not seller support.

**Enforcement mechanisms:**

- **Pro Seller application review.** Walmart manually reviews insurance certificates, tax forms, and business licenses before approving Pro Seller status. Rejection rate is approximately 40% for first-time applicants.
- **WFS inventory acceptance.** Walmart Fulfillment Services rejects inbound shipments if the supplier invoice, packing list, and UPC do not match the purchase order. One electronics seller reported 22% of their first WFS shipment was rejected due to supplier invoice discrepancies.
- **Category-specific compliance audits.** Walmart's Product Safety team periodically requests test reports and certificates for active SKUs. If you cannot provide them within 7 days, the SKU is delisted.

**Where verification gaps cost you:**

- **Pro Seller rejection.** Without a valid insurance certificate naming Walmart as additional insured, your application is auto-rejected and you cannot access WFS, Walmart Connect, or Pro Seller support.
- **Inbound shipment rejection fees.** WFS charges $25 per carton rejection fee, plus return shipping. A 500-unit shipment (20 cartons) costs $500 in rejection fees alone.
- **Margin erosion from manual compliance work.** One apparel brand spent $12,000 per year on a compliance freelancer to manage Walmart certificate renewals and test report uploads because their supplier did not provide organized documentation.

- **Third-party lab test reports** (for supplements, cosmetics, electronics). TikTok Shop accepts ISO 17025-accredited labs and requires reports dated within the last 12 months.
- **Supplier business license** (for all sellers). TikTok Shop requires a valid business license from the country of origin.
- **Brand authorization letter** (if you are reselling branded products). TikTok Shop enforces this through its IP Protection Program.
- **Proof of product liability insurance** (for high-risk categories). TikTok Shop may request this during category qualification review.

**Enforcement mechanisms:**

- **Seller verification at onboarding.** TikTok Shop requires business license, tax ID, and bank account verification before you can list products. Video-based identity verification is required for individual sellers.

**Where verification gaps cost you:**

## How to Build a Multi-Platform Supplier Verification System

A multi-platform supplier verification system must store documents, track expiry dates, and map each document to the specific platform enforcement mechanism that will check it.

**Step 1: Centralize supplier documents in a compliance hub.**

Create a folder structure for each supplier with subfolders for:

- Business registration (license, articles of incorporation, tax ID)
- Quality certifications (ISO 9001, cGMP, BRC)
- Insurance (general liability, product liability)
- Testing and inspection (lab reports, CPSC certificates, FDA registration)
- Financial and identity (DUNS number, bank account verification, INFORM Act documentation)

Store documents in a shared drive with version control and expiry date tracking. Use a spreadsheet or compliance software to track:

- Document name
- Supplier name
- Issue date
- Expiry date
- Platform requirement (Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop)
- Category (supplements, children's products, electronics)
- Status (valid, expiring in 30 days, expired)

**Step 2: Map documents to platform-specific enforcement mechanisms.**

For each SKU, create a compliance checklist that maps required documents to the platform enforcement system:

**Example: Supplement SKU on Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop**

| Platform | Enforcement System | Required Documents | Expiry Check |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Amazon | Account Health → Product Safety | cGMP audit, NSF test report, FDA registration | Every 12 months |
| Walmart | Pro Seller compliance audit | Lab test report, product liability insurance, W-9 | Insurance: annual; test report: 12 months |
| TikTok Shop | Category qualification review | Lab test report, FDA registration, business license | Test report: 12 months; FDA: continuous |

This table becomes your SKU launch checklist. Before listing a product on a new platform, verify that all required documents are valid and uploaded.

**Step 3: Automate expiry tracking and renewal reminders.**

Set up calendar reminders 60 days before document expiry. For high-risk documents (insurance, lab test reports, cGMP audits), set a 90-day reminder to allow time for supplier coordination and renewal processing.

Use a compliance tool or Airtable base with automation rules:

- If expiry date is within 90 days, send email to procurement team
- If expiry date is within 30 days, escalate to operations manager
- If document expires, flag all affected SKUs and pause new inventory orders

**Step 4: Require supplier documentation as part of purchase order terms.**

Update your purchase order terms to require suppliers to provide:

- Valid business license and tax registration
- Quality management certificate (ISO 9001 or equivalent)
- Product liability insurance naming your company as additional insured
- Lab test reports for all regulated categories
- Written confirmation that products comply with all applicable U.S. regulations (CPSC, FDA, FTC)

Make payment of the final invoice contingent on receipt of all required documents. One apparel brand reduced compliance delays by 60% by adding a clause that withholds 10% of the invoice until supplier documents are uploaded to their shared drive.

**Step 5: Conduct supplier audits before scaling to new platforms.**

Before listing an existing product on a new platform, audit your supplier documentation against the new platform's requirements.

**Example audit checklist for adding TikTok Shop:**

- Do we have a valid lab test report dated within the last 12 months?
- Is our supplier's business license current and uploaded to our compliance hub?
- If this is a children's product, do we have CPSC tracking labels and certificates of compliance?
- If this is a cosmetic, is our supplier's facility registered with FDA?
- Do we have a brand authorization letter if we are reselling a branded product?

If any document is missing or expired, pause the TikTok Shop launch until documents are obtained. Launching with incomplete documentation creates a 30 to 90 day payout hold and suspension risk.

## Red Flags That Indicate Weak Supplier Verification

Weak supplier verification shows up as operational friction before it shows up as platform enforcement.

**Red flag 1: Your supplier cannot provide a lab test report within 48 hours.**

If your supplier says "we have a test report but it takes a week to find it," they do not have an organized quality management system. This means they also cannot quickly provide updated reports when regulations change or when a platform requests additional testing.

**Red flag 2: Your supplier provides a test report that is more than 12 months old.**

Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop require test reports dated within the last 12 months for most regulated categories. A 2-year-old report does not satisfy platform requirements and will be rejected during compliance review.

**Red flag 3: Your supplier refuses to name you as additional insured on their product liability insurance.**

Walmart Pro Seller requires product liability insurance naming Walmart as additional insured. If your supplier refuses to add you as additional insured, they are either underinsured or do not have insurance. This blocks Walmart Pro Seller approval and creates legal risk if a product defect triggers a lawsuit.

**Red flag 4: Your supplier does not have a DUNS number.**

Amazon requires FDA registration and DUNS numbers for food and supplement imports. If your supplier does not have a DUNS number, they have never supplied to a large retailer or government entity and may not meet basic business verification standards.

**Red flag 5: Your supplier provides a business license that is not in English and refuses to provide a certified translation.**

Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop require business licenses in English or with certified translation. If your supplier refuses to provide this, they are either not legally registered or do not understand U.S. marketplace requirements.

**Red flag 6: Your supplier cannot provide a certificate of compliance for a children's product.**

CPSC requires a certificate of compliance for all children's products. If your supplier says "we have never been asked for this," they have never supplied to the U.S. market or have only supplied to platforms with weak enforcement. This is a suspension risk on Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between supplier verification and product testing?**

Supplier verification confirms that the supplier is a legitimate, compliant business (business license, tax registration, insurance, quality certifications). Product testing confirms that the product meets safety and regulatory standards (lab test reports, CPSC certificates, FDA registration). Both are required. Supplier verification happens at the business level. Product testing happens at the SKU level.

**How often do I need to renew supplier documents?**

Business licenses and tax registrations are typically annual. Quality certifications (ISO 9001, cGMP) are renewed every 1 to 3 years depending on the standard. Insurance certificates are annual. Lab test reports are valid for 12 months for most platforms. CPSC certificates are valid until the product changes. FDA registration is annual. Track all expiry dates and set reminders 90 days before expiry.

**Can I use the same supplier documents for Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop?**

Most documents (business license, ISO 9001, lab test reports) can be reused across platforms if they meet each platform's specific requirements. However, some documents are platform-specific. Walmart requires insurance naming Walmart as additional insured. TikTok Shop requires CPSC eFiling confirmation for children's products. Amazon requires NSF testing for high-risk supplement categories. Always audit documents against each platform's requirements before listing.

**What happens if my supplier document expires after I list the product?**

Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop may suspend the SKU or your seller account if they detect an expired document during a compliance audit. Set reminders 90 days before expiry and renew documents proactively. If a document expires, pause new inventory orders and update the document before the platform flags it. One supplement brand had 12 SKUs suspended on Amazon because their cGMP audit expired and they did not upload a renewed certificate within 30 days of expiry.

**How do I verify that a supplier's test report is legitimate?**

Request the original test report on lab letterhead with the lab's contact information, accreditation number, and testing date. Verify that the lab is ISO 17025-accredited by searching the lab name on the American Association for Laboratory Accreditation (A2LA) or International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation (ILAC) database. Call the lab to confirm that they issued the report and that it covers your specific product. If the supplier refuses to provide the lab's contact information or the lab cannot confirm the report, the report is likely fake.
